    How the loads are calculated

    Loads and material

    • Partial factors 1.35 on self weight and 1.5 on imposed load, with a dynamic factor of 1.2 for motor-hoisted trusses, as used in EN 17115.
    • AL 6082-T6 tube: fo 250, fu 290 MPa. Welded zones: fo,haz 125, fu,haz 185 MPa (EN 1999-1-1 Table 3.2b). γM1 1.10, γM2 1.25.
    • Simply supported single span. Point loads at nodes.
    • Deflection: mid-span sag under the imposed load, without safety factors, with the shear deformation of the bracing included. Limit L/200. Self-weight sag is shown separately.

    What is checked along the span

    • Chords: compression with flexural buckling between nodes (class A curve) and the welded-zone section; tension at welded sections.
    • Spigot joint: pin double shear, pin bearing on connector walls, spigot shear-out and net section, and the welded zone where the connector meets the chord.
    • Braces: buckling and welded-zone capacity of each diagonal and post.
    • Open top face: where the top face has no diagonals, the top chords are checked for buckling sideways, held only by the post and cross-tube frames (joint stiffness taken as half of fully rigid).
    • Brace welds to chord: chord face yielding and punching shear (EN 1993-1-8 §7 gap K-joint, with fo,haz). Reduced where the chord is already in compression.

    Taken from the drawings

    • 400 × 400 is X-braced (Ø25 × 3 at 45°) with Ø40 × 3 posts at 470 mm, not single diagonals.
    • 600 × 525 side faces have Ø30 × 3 diagonals with Ø30 × 3 posts at about 940 mm; Ø50 × 3 chords on the 1, 2 and 3 m.
    • 600 × 525 · Ø60 × 4: product chart Sheet 33. Chords 540 × 465 apart, nodes every 467 mm, top face with cross tubes only.
    • The 60 mm connector has a Ø50 / Ø45 stub (2.5 mm wall). Butt-welded to a Ø50 chord it is the weakest part of the joint, at 55 kN.
    • 800 × 550: Ø60 × 4 chords at 740 × 490 centres, Ø30 × 3 diagonals and posts, from drawings H_800x550_2m and 3m.
    • 760 × 520 (60 and 50 mm) and 800 × 610: from the product chart sheets 15, 25 and 26 (BOM, chord centres, brace angles). Their top faces have cross tubes but no diagonals.
    • 1100 × 650 (Ø60 × 4 and Ø60 × 6): drawings VST_1100_650_1/2/3MTR, product chart Sheet 34. Chords 1040 × 590 apart, side braces Ø30 × 3 at 68.9°, bottom braces at 56.1°. The Ø60 × 6 version changes only the main tube wall.
    • 480 × 480: weights from product chart Sheet 27; bracing assumed the same as the 490 × 490 · Ø60 × 4 until the drawing is checked.
    • Pin steel is not on the drawings. EN-19 (fu 850 MPa) is assumed, as listed on the product chart.
